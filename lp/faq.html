Landing page FAQ

Short answers. Email to book now — pay when live — HTML same week.

What’s the turnaround?

Same week after you send product name, one-line offer, price, and optional screenshots. Flagship ($449) is usually 1–2 weeks (outline first).

What do I send?

Product name, one-sentence offer, price, CTA text, 2–8 screenshots/logo. Testimonials optional. That’s enough to start.

Which tier should I pick?

$99 — one screen, one CTA (single SKU). $149 — most booked (proof + FAQ + dual CTA). $249 — features + pricing block. $449 — custom map + comparison + three revisions.

Do I get the HTML?

Yes — one self-contained HTML file. Host on itch, Netlify, GitHub Pages, or your domain.

How do revisions work?

$99/$149 include one revision pass. $249 includes two. $449 includes three. Bigger scope jumps up a tier.

How do I pay?

PayPal.Me or Interac. Memo: Pixel & Pitch — LP $[tier]. Email starts the booking now; pay buttons light up when URLs are wired (or I email the link same day).

What kinds of offers work?

Indie tools, asset shops, digital products, courses, services — anything with one clear offer and a hard CTA. Same one-file HTML handoff.
